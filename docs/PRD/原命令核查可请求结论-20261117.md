## 改前框的通用映射

Fund known false→S号/U；known audit→S号；known receipt→S原；rejected audit→R/S号；never→空；A7覆盖audit为never。Command known false→S号/N/U；audit→S号/N；receipt→S原/N；rejected→R/S号/N；never→N。E2/E6不传known，所有状态都落S号/N/U。closureOpen原来仅排除PROCESSING，过期未查/查无/读取出错也开框，提交必拒。
